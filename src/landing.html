FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how the platform works.

What is Smart Health Resource Allocator?

A decision-support dashboard for NGO field teams. It ranks villages by medical vulnerability so maternal, infant and child health camps can reach the highest-barrier areas first.

How is the priority score calculated?

A transparent 0 to 100 weighted score: flood risk 40%, facility distance 25%, BHU absence 15%, road accessibility 10% and verified population 10%. Missing fields are excluded, never guessed.

Where does the data come from?

From the supplied village dataset plus records your team adds through manual entry or CSV/Excel bulk upload. Live WHO indicators in the AI assistant are shown as reference only and never change the scores.

Does the tool replace field verification?

No. It supports planning. The final allocation decision always remains with the NGO officer.

How do I add new villages?

Log in, open Log field data, and either fill the form or upload a CSV/Excel file. Rankings update immediately.

Do I need an account?

You can read this page without one. To use the dashboard, create an account from the login screen.
